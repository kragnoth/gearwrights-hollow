Gearwright's Hollow

A semi-sandbox pack for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge.

The world has been abandoned for a while. Villages are empty, roads are overgrown and most of the old buildings are falling apart. You start with nothing and rebuild using Create, Immersive Engineering, Ender IO and a bit of blood magic.

The pack is low magic and leans toward old machinery. Nothing is locked behind quests. They're there to point you in a direction if you want one.

What's in it

  • Hand-built ruined structures: villages, chapels, mine heads, outposts and a few stranger places
  • Around 260 quests in 15 chapters
  • A large skill tree with perks you unlock as you level
  • Gear that rolls quality, plus named unique items to find
  • Tougher mobs that can spawn with extra abilities
  • Farming and cooking through Farmer's Delight and its add-ons
  • An endgame that ties into the world's darker side

Several mods were made for this pack: Hallmarked, Wasteland Vision, Mob Rules, Kragnoth's Skill Perks, Tarbound, Rust and Rebar and Cog & Cultivar.

Requirements

  • Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.262, Java 21
  • 8 GB of RAM allocated is recommended

Multiplayer

The pack works on servers. Everyone's progress is tracked separately.

Bugs

This is still a release candidate. If something breaks, please post your latest.log (found in the logs folder) with your report.
